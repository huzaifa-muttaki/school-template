# Image Licences

Every photograph in `public/images/` is listed below. Each one was retrieved from Wikimedia Commons
with the licence recorded on its file page at download time (October 2026), and each is released
under **CC0 1.0** (public-domain dedication) or marked **Public Domain**. Both permit commercial
use, modification and redistribution without attribution; creators are credited here anyway as good practice.

Files are served as `<asset>-640.webp`, `<asset>-1280.webp` and `<asset>-1920.webp`. Each was resized
and given a light colour grade (−10% saturation, +4% contrast) for consistency.

Selection rules applied to every image:

- No identifiable people (two images contain fully out-of-focus figures, noted below).
- No logos, brand marks, school or university names, crests, uniforms or readable signage.
- No copyrighted artwork as a subject.
- Real buildings are shown only as generic architecture. Captions and alt text describe the
  **fictional** Nireka campus and never name the real location.
